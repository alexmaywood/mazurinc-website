Truckstopbeer — App Support

Truckstopbeer is a community-powered map of truck stops, reported beer availability and amenities, designed for adult drivers using Android phones or iPhones.

Contact support

For help with the app, incorrect stop information, a technical issue, privacy concerns or a community report, email:

contact@mazurinc.com

Include your device model, Android or iOS version, a brief description of the issue and approximate time it occurred. Do not email passwords, authentication tokens or sensitive documents.

Common questions

Why can't I vote?

Location permission and a recent, accurate GPS fix near the truck stop are required. Check that the app has location permission and that precise/high-accuracy location is enabled, move to an open area and use the My Location control.

Why isn't a truck stop shown?

Map coverage depends partly on OpenStreetMap and community data and may be incomplete. Do not submit a vote for an unrelated location.

Are the reports guaranteed accurate?

No. Reports are community-submitted, can be outdated or incorrect and do not guarantee product availability.

How can I request correction or deletion?

Email contact@mazurinc.com with your request. In-app anonymous-account deletion is not yet available in the pre-release version; requests will be reviewed while a safe deletion process is developed.

Availability on Android and iPhone

Truckstopbeer is being prepared for distribution through Google Play and the Apple App Store. Store listing links will be added after each app is approved and publicly available. This page does not indicate that either store has approved or published the app.

Safety

For adults age 21 and older. Never drink and drive. Follow applicable laws. Routes are for planning only and are not truck-specific turn-by-turn navigation.

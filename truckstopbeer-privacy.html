Truckstopbeer Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Operator: Mazur Global LLC
Contact: contact@mazurinc.com

This policy describes the current planned iPhone application and the services used to provide community truck-stop information. Practices will be reviewed again before public App Store release.

Information we process

Truckstopbeer uses an anonymous Supabase account identifier and authentication session to manage contributions without asking for a public name, email address or password. Beer and amenity reports, place suggestions and correction reports are associated with that identifier in the backend. Shared community results, stop locations, vote counts and confirmation times may be visible to other users.

Location

With your permission, foreground location is used to show nearby stops, center and follow the map, and check that a report is submitted near the stop. When you vote, the app sends location coordinates, location accuracy and capture time to its backend for validation. The app does not request background location or intentionally create a personal travel-history feature. Network providers may retain operational request logs.

Search, maps and routing

Map display, discovery, address search and route requests use Supabase, Apple Maps and configured OpenStreetMap-related search/routing providers. Requests may disclose IP addresses, search terms and selected route endpoints to those providers, subject to their own practices.

Information stored on your device

Age self-confirmation and display preferences are stored locally. Anonymous session credentials are protected using iOS Keychain. The app does not request a date of birth or documentary proof of age.

Advertising and tracking

The iPhone version described here does not include an advertising SDK or cross-app tracking. This does not mean that no data is collected: community contributions and anonymous account identifiers are stored to provide the service.

Retention and requests

Community confirmation records and their timestamps do not automatically expire. Retention of provider-side operational logs depends on the respective services. For questions, access, correction or deletion requests, email contact@mazurinc.com. We will review requests and explain the options available. Uninstalling the app does not automatically delete backend records.

Pre-release notice: A safe in-app anonymous-account deletion mechanism and final retention procedures are still being developed. This page must be reviewed and updated before App Store submission; it does not claim that in-app account deletion is currently available.

Updates

This policy may change as the app and its service providers change. The effective date will be updated when material changes are published.
